#snippets
### Insert image HTML
```html
<center>
<img src=/path width=55% height=55%>
</center>
```

### Insert Video HTML
```html
<center>
<video width=20% height=20% controls>
  <source src="/path.mov">
</video>
</center>
```

### Insert left-aligned LaTeX Block
$$
\begin{flalign}
& \text{1st line} & \\

& \text{2nd Line} \\

& \text{3rd Line} \\

& \text{final line}
\end{flalign}
$$
### Insert center-aligned LaTeX Block
$$
\begin{gathered}
\text{1st line} \\\\
\text{longer 2nd line} \\\\
\text{even way longer final line}
\end{gathered}
$$

### Insert manual-aligned LaTeX Block

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{1st line} &= \text{1st line} \\\\

\text{2md line} &= \text{even longer 2nd line} \\\\

\text{final line} &= \text{even way longer final line than above}
\end{aligned}
$$
